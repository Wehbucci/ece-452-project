# Team Contract

## Roles

- **Hasan** is our Team Lead, responsible for coordinating the project, keeping the team on track, defining meeting agendas, and posting weekly meeting notes and summaries.
- **Andria** is our Technical Lead, responsible for ensuring technical quality, reviewing code, enforcing best practices, and helping team members debug and resolve technical blockers.
- **Ali** is our Design Lead, responsible for guiding UI/UX decisions, maintaining visual consistency, and overseeing the overall look and feel of the product.
- **Leo** is our Motivation Lead, responsible for keeping team morale high, celebrating wins, and making sure nobody loses the will to live mid-sprint.
- **All members** contribute to both frontend and backend development, share wallet-burning expenses equally, and are expected to wear multiple hats as the project demands.

> Note: Roles are not rigid. Everyone is expected to contribute across the stack, and significant decisions are made collectively — no one person is "the boss."

---

## Communication

- Our primary communication channel is our **WhatsApp group** for day-to-day messages, quick updates, and informal coordination.
- For longer discussions, sharing files, or code-related topics, we will use our project repository and any agreed supplementary tools (e.g., GitHub Issues, comments).

---

## Meetings

- We will hold a **weekly virtual meeting every Wednesday from 5:30 PM to 6:30 PM**.
- All members will attend or notify the team **in advance** of any anticipated absence. Absences should be rare and communicated as early as possible.
- **Hasan** will take meeting notes and circulate a written summary to the group **within 24 hours** after each meeting.
- All members will come to meetings prepared by:
  - Reading any shared materials or relevant course content ahead of time,
  - Reviewing any open tasks, PRs, or issues assigned to them, and
  - Coming with ideas or questions relevant to the agenda.
- Members will not work on unrelated assignments during meetings and will remain engaged for the full hour.
- Decisions can be made during meetings even if some members are absent, as long as the meeting was announced in advance and notes are shared afterward.
- Each member will practice active listening. One person speaks at a time, and everyone's input is heard before decisions are finalized.

---

## Team Expectations

- **Commitment:** All members are expected to contribute equitably to the project. If a member is overwhelmed or blocked, they should communicate this early rather than going silent.
- **Deadlines:** Members should complete their assigned tasks by agreed internal deadlines. If you anticipate missing a deadline, flag it in the WhatsApp group as soon as possible so the team can adapt.
- **Code quality:** All code contributions should follow agreed-upon conventions, be reasonably documented, and go through a review process before merging.
- **Respect:** All members will treat each other with respect in meetings and in written communication. Critique ideas, not people.
- **Accountability:** If you say you'll do something, do it. If you can't, say so early.

---

## Conflict Management

- Conflicts are expected and are a normal part of teamwork. We treat them as **allies working toward a shared goal**, not opponents competing against each other.
- Our preferred approach to conflict is **collaboration and problem-solving**: we confront issues directly, with a constructive attitude, and look for outcomes that work for everyone.
- When a disagreement arises:
  1. The involved members will first attempt to resolve it between themselves.
  2. If unresolved, the issue is brought to the full team at the next meeting (or sooner if urgent).
  3. The team discusses and reaches a consensus. Hasan, as Team Lead, facilitates but does not have final authority as decisions are made collectively.
  4. If consensus cannot be reached, the team will vote, with majority ruling.
- No member should feel afraid to voice disagreement. Silence is not consensus.
